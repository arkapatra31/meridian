Why it's faster
Without Meridian, the agent must discover the codebase as it goes — spawning sub-agents, opening files, and tracing imports to answer structural questions. With Meridian, the knowledge graph is pre-built: hub nodes, call chains, module relationships, and cluster membership are immediately queryable. The exploration phase disappears. The agent goes straight from question to answer.
